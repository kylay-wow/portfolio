PROJECT / 002 SUSTAINABILITY CONCEPT

HydroLink Corridor↗

HydroLink Corridor is a sustainability concept developed for the CDL-GCNS Young SDG Leaders Award 2026 as part of a four-member team. It proposes sheltered pedestrian corridors that integrate atmospheric water harvesting, greenery and solar-powered fans to improve urban comfort and environmental efficiency.

PROJECT / 003 HACKATHON CONCEPT

Defend-the-box↗

Defend-the-box is a hackathon concept that gamifies real-world scam scenarios. Players manage a virtual bank account and make decisions to protect their savings from different scams. AI-powered family member consultations provide hints and generate personalised scam scenarios based on different player backgrounds.

I collaborated with teammates to brainstorm the game concept and explore ways to make scam awareness more engaging and interactive.
